Open Compendium VERSION update
==============================

1. Close Open Compendium.
2. Extract this zip into your Open Compendium folder (the one with "Open Compendium.exe" in it).
   When Windows asks, choose "Replace the files in the destination".
3. Start the program. The version at the foot of the Start page should say VERSION.

Only the program's own files (resources\app, README.txt and Libraries\open-compendium-core.part,
the core library that comes with the program) are replaced.
Your characters, pictures, own library files and settings are not touched.
